For students

A simple way to revise each topic.

Open the notes, choose SL or HL at the top, and pick a topic. Then follow the line.

  1. STOP 1

    Understand it

    Read the topic. If a part feels hard, switch to the textbook version or watch the short video.

  2. STOP 2

    Remember it

    Go through the topic's flashcards in Learn mode until they're all mastered.

  3. STOP 3

    Use it

    Answer a few practice questions and mark yourself honestly with the markscheme.

  4. STOP 4

    Get exam-ready

    Closer to the exam, work through the specimen papers and try a timed paper.

Your progress saves on your device by itself. Press / anywhere to search the notes, the glossary and every question.

Questions

Good to know.

Is it really free?

Yes. No payments, no ads and no account. Just open it and study.

Where is my progress saved?

In your browser, on the device you're using. Nobody else can see it, and your laptop and phone keep separate progress.

Which syllabus does it follow?

IB Diploma Computer Science with first exams in 2027, both SL and HL: Theme A (computer fundamentals, networks, databases, machine learning) and Theme B (computational thinking, programming, object-oriented programming, and abstract data types for HL). Code examples come in Python and Java.

Is this official IB material?

No. These are independent study notes, not made by, endorsed by or connected to the International Baccalaureate Organization. "IB" and "International Baccalaureate" are its trademarks. Always check against the official subject guide and your teacher.

I found a mistake. What do I do?

Thank you. Please send a correction with the topic code (for example A2.1) and what's wrong. Ideas are welcome too: make a suggestion. Both need a free GitHub account.

Can I share it?

Please do. Send the link to classmates and teachers. The notes are © d1v4nchik, all rights reserved, so share the link rather than copying, reposting or selling the content.
